Nesource AG, headquartered in Zug, is the exclusive distributor of the renewable fuels produced by Helvoil SA. As the commercial and logistical arm of the group, Nesource ensures that high-quality HVO, SAF and other renewable fuels reach customers throughout Switzerland efficiently and reliably.

To meet the rapidly growing demand for sustainable energy solutions — and to support Switzerland in achieving its climate and substitution targets — Nesource will not only market Helvoil's domestic production but will also import renewable fuels from international producers. This includes HVO, SAF and other advanced biofuels, sourced from certified and traceable supply chains.

By combining Swiss production with international procurement, Nesource guarantees a stable, diversified and compliant supply for road transport, marine, aviation, and industrial customers. The company works closely with fuel distributors, transport operators, and corporate clients to support their decarbonisation strategies and ensure adherence to Swiss sustainability obligations.

As the dedicated distribution company of the Helvoil Group, Nesource plays a central role in strengthening Switzerland's security of supply in renewable fuels and in accelerating the transition toward a low-carbon mobility system.
